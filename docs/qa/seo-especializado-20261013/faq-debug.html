A working demo, a concrete pilot, a release to own

For funded pre-seed and seed founders without a CTO, with a business pilot or paying users in view. I inventory the backend, integrations and account ownership before proposing architecture changes. Leaving Lovable is not a prerequisite.

What I need to understand

  • Does the app use Lovable Cloud, connected Supabase or a custom backend?
  • Which roles can view, change, approve and export each organization’s data?
  • Are payments native or custom? Which event grants access or fulfills an order?
  • Which existing scan findings and eligible fixes have been reviewed for this version?

Repair or rebuild?

Retain and repair

I retain the prototype when its core workflow exists, business rules are defined and the required changes fit a bounded delivery. I agree checks for legitimate access, tenant isolation and failure recovery where relevant to the scope.

Rescope before execution

Missing core workflows, an unsuitable data model or integrations that need rebuilding require a new scope before execution. New features and reconstruction are not automatically part of production preparation.

What makes delivery verifiable

This is the acceptance structure I adapt to your project.

Roles and data
A user × action × data matrix with evidence for agreed allowed and denied access scenarios.
Core workflow
Success, failure and repeated-action scenarios tested with synthetic data in an authorized environment.
Integrations
Relevant responses, failure paths and events checked; payment and entitlement acceptance agreed when billing is involved.
Operations
Release, rollback, backup/restore and monitoring procedures defined and verified within the contracted scope.

Before we talk

Do I need to leave Lovable?

Not necessarily. The workflow, dependencies and operating responsibilities guide that decision. A migration needs a reason and its own scope.

Do native scans already check logic and permissions?

Yes. Quick and Deep have different coverage; Deep includes logic, authorization and payments. I use their findings and eligible fixes, then agree business acceptance with you.

Are you official support or a certified partner?

No. I provide independent engineering. Account and platform issues belong with official support; I do not claim a partnership or certification.
